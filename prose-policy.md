# Public text

Every public sentence must make sense to a reader who sees only this repository or website.

State mechanisms and facts directly.

Use one fact per sentence.

Keep titles as nouns or noun phrases.

Do not add subtitles.

Do not describe the editing process, the chat, or rejected approaches.

Use evidence for research claims.

Keep exact publication titles in citation records.

Use ASCII name forms for display and preserve source spellings in the research evidence.

Keep author order exact.

Record source conflicts in `SOURCES.md`.
